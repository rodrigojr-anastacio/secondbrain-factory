DESCRIPTION
In March 1946, a Navy commodore asked 167 people to give up their island "for the good of mankind." Then the United States tested 23 nuclear weapons there. Eighty years later, the people of Bikini still can't go home to stay.
We follow the paper trail: the goodbye that was staged for the cameras, the bomb that missed, the aircraft carrier that sank with its planes still aboard, the hydrogen bomb that was far bigger than planned, the island declared "safe" in 1968, and the $563 million award that was 99.6% unpaid.

If someone in your family served at Operation Crossroads, on a target ship, a support ship, or in the air, tell us their name and their ship.
