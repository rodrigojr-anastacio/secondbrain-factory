## Descrição do canal (en, rascunho)
> Places America fought for that you're not allowed to visit, and the paperwork that explains why. Every video takes one famous military place, reads the government's own records on screen, and shows what is still there today. Archive photos, film and documents from the U.S. National Archives, the Naval History and Heritage Command, DVIDS, the National Park Service and the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, credited in every description.
