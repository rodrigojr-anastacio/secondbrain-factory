# Fact-check notes for the French pilot

- J.R.R. Tolkien, *The Lord of the Rings*, Appendix B, “The Tale of Years”: the Fourth Age entry for Sam's departure after Rose's death and his status as the last Ring-bearer to pass West. The narrative is reported as a tradition kept by Elanor; the text does not stage a reunion with Frodo.
- J.R.R. Tolkien, *The Return of the King*, Book VI, Chapter 9, “The Grey Havens”: Frodo notes that Sam was also a Ring-bearer, briefly, and hints that Sam may one day follow.
- J.R.R. Tolkien, Letter 325: Aman does not make mortal visitors immortal; mortals are permitted there only for a limited stay.

The narration paraphrases these points and avoids claiming that Tolkien depicts Sam and Frodo's reunion. Verify the wording against the edition you use before publishing.
