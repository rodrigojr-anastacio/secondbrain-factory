Right now, under the streets of Okinawa, Japan, American bombs from 1945 are still waiting to be found. In March, a construction crew dug one up at the foot of a famous ridge: 550 pounds, 81 years in the ground, still armed. To make it safe, the city cleared 849 people out of their homes and shops. The ridge above it is the one from the movie: Hacksaw Ridge.

In this video: the medic who wouldn't carry a gun and lowered 75 wounded men down the cliff on a rope, the 20-year-old student from Naha who was inside the ridge on the other side, the day Truman hung the Medal of Honor around Desmond Doss's neck (on real film), why the movie wasn't filmed there, the men who came back, and the ridge today: homes on its slopes, a monorail station at its foot, and a war that keeps coming out of its ground.

How we made this video: we wrote it from Desmond Doss's Medal of Honor citation, his 1995 interview, the Army's official history and Okinawa's bomb reports. The 3D shots of the ridge, the maps and the graphics were made for this video. Credits below.
