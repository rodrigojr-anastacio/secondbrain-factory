Robert E. Lee surrendered his army in Wilmer McLean's parlor on 9 April 1865. That afternoon is only part of McLean's story.

The record follows him from Yorkshire and his wartime business to the disputed souvenirs from the surrender room, Bettie and Mary Ann and their families, and the house that was dismantled, left on the spot and rebuilt decades later.
