In February 1956, a man born before the Civil War went on CBS's I've Got a Secret. His secret: when he was five, he saw John Wilkes Booth shoot Abraham Lincoln. Samuel J. Seymour told the story in a Sunday magazine in 1954 and on television in 1956, and on the day of the show he told a Washington paper that Lincoln stood up and fell, though Lincoln was sitting. Ford's Theatre looked into his story in 2018 and wrote that we will likely never know if he was there. He died two months after the show, and was buried on the 91st anniversary of the night Lincoln was shot.
