For Veterans Day: in May 1862, Robert Smalls, enslaved and twenty-three, put on the captain's straw hat and sailed the Confederate steamer Planter out of Charleston, right past Fort Sumter, and handed her to the Union Navy under a white bedsheet, with sixteen people on board, his wife and children among them. By 1865 he was her captain. Later he served five terms in Congress, and in 1895 he told South Carolina's convention he would ask no one else to let him remain.
