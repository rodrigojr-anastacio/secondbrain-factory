This is the only known photograph of Abraham Lincoln dead, taken in New York City Hall on 24 April 1865. The Secretary of War ordered the plates seized and destroyed. He kept one print, and a fourteen-year-old found it in Springfield 87 years later. The rest of the pictures of Lincoln's death, and which ones are real, are in the long on the channel.
